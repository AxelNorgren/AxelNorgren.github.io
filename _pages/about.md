I am a PhD student in Economics at [Linköping University](https://liu.se/en), based at the [Institute for Analytical Sociology (IAS)](https://liu.se/en/organisation/liu/iei/ias) in Norrköping. I am also an affiliated researcher at the [Center of Educational Leadership and Excellence (CELE)](https://www.hhs.se/en/research/institutes/siir/cele/) at the Stockholm School of Economics, where my main supervisor is Professor Karl Wennberg.

My research sits at the intersection of **economics of education** and **teacher economics**. I study how schools, teachers, and policies shape student and teacher outcomes — with a particular focus on the Swedish context and using administrative register data.

In early 2026 I was a visiting PhD student at the **London School of Economics** (Department of Geography and Environment), hosted by Professor Olmo Silva.

## Working Papers

**Effect of Student Teaching Placements on Teacher Career Development**  
*Axel Norgren*  
I study how school placements affect student teachers' probability of completing their education and securing employment post-graduation, as well as what type of school they begin their career in.

**Effectiveness of School Inspections** *(updated version)*  
*Axel Norgren*  
An update of my earlier policy brief, using a doubly-robust difference-in-differences model to study the effect of Swedish School Inspectorate visits on educational outcomes up to six years post-inspection.

**Models of Peer Effects in Education**  
*Axel Norgren, Karl Wennberg*  
A review of theoretical models of peer effects in education, their empirical support, and implications for research design and education policy.
