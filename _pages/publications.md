## Working Papers

**Effect of Student Teaching Placements on Teacher Career Development**  
Axel Norgren  
I study how the placement of student teachers affects their probability of completing teacher education, obtaining a job post-graduation, and the type of school they begin their career in.

---

**Effectiveness of School Inspections**  
Axel Norgren  
An updated and extended version of my earlier policy brief. Using a doubly-robust difference-in-differences design, I estimate the causal effect of Swedish School Inspectorate visits on school-level educational outcomes up to six years post-inspection.

---

**Models of Peer Effects in Education**  
Axel Norgren, Karl Wennberg  
We review theoretical models of peer effects in education, assess the empirical evidence for each model, and discuss implications for research design and education policy.  
*EdWorking Papers – Annenberg Institute for School Reform at Brown University* (working paper version of the Arora-Jonsson et al. study also available here)

## Published

**Seeking opportunity or socio-economic status? Housing and school choice in Sweden**  
Andersson, F., Mutgan, S., **Norgren, A.**, Wennberg, K. (2025)  
*Urban Studies*, 62(5), 367–386.  
We examine all families in Sweden whose children approach primary school age and explore residential mobility across school catchment areas. Families appear to use the housing market as an instrument for school choice, moving to areas with higher-SES pupils and higher test scores — but schools themselves provide little additional value-added.

**Beyond the silver bullet: unveiling multiple pathways to school turnaround**  
Arora-Jonsson, S., Demir, E., **Norgren, A.**, Wennberg, K. (2024)  
*School Effectiveness and School Improvement*, 1–36.  
Using qualitative comparative analysis (QCA) across 77 Swedish schools over 10 years, we identify necessary and sufficient conditions for school turnaround — highlighting its causally complex, context-dependent nature.

---

**Dynamics of founding team diversity and venture outcomes: A simulation approach**  
Levesque, M., Wennberg, K., Sundriyal, V.K., **Norgren, A.** (2024)  
*Strategic Entrepreneurship Journal*, 1–51.  
We build a simulation model of how founding team diversity affects performance and disruption over time. Greater diversity increases the likelihood of high-performing ventures but also raises disruption risk — a double-edged sword.

**Stärker Skolinspektionen kvalitén i svensk skola?**  
**Norgren, A.** (2022)  
*Ekonomisk debatt*, 50(8): 43–53.  
A quasi-experimental study of Swedish School Inspectorate visits between 2017–2021. Results indicate a negligible effect on schools' educational performance.
