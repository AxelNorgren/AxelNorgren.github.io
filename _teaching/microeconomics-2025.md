Undergraduate course in microeconomics at Linköping University, autumn 2025. The course covers core microeconomic theory with a focus on market structure and international trade.

## Responsibilities

**Lectures** — Delivered lectures to a class of 100+ students, covering:
- Imperfect competition (monopoly, oligopoly, monopolistic competition)
- Trade economics (comparative advantage, gains from trade, trade policy)

**Problem sets** — Led problem set sessions where students worked through and presented solutions to applied microeconomic problems. Also responsible for grading group assignments and written exam questions.
